⟶ Schedule A — Licensed Fiendish Activities ⟵
✦ A-001
Unsolicited Hat Commentary

The bearer is licensed to comment on the hat situation of any server member at any time, including members who are not wearing hats and really should be. Comments must be delivered with gravity befitting a licensed cult operative. "Nice hat" is permitted. "That hat lacks structure and I am disappointed" is also permitted. "Give me that hat" is a grey area currently under review.

✦ A-002
Spontaneous Hat-Related Derailment

Any ongoing discussion — about games, politics, the economy, someone's weekend — may be legally redirected toward hats by a Cult of Hats member at any point. The transition must be smooth. "That reminds me of a hat I saw once" is smooth. "Anyway, hats" is also acceptable. Abrupt pivots are permitted but must be followed by a meaningful hat anecdote or the licence is briefly suspended for three (3) minutes.

✦ A-003
Hat GIF Deployment in Inopportune Moments

Members are licensed to deploy hat GIFs during serious conversations, heated debates, or moments of genuine emotional sincerity, provided the GIF is truly excellent. A mediocre hat GIF in an inopportune moment is a fiendish act in the wrong direction. Standards must be maintained. The bar for "truly excellent" is set by Darkneblade personally, which means the bar is very high and also shaped like a brim.

✦ A-004
Ceremonial Tipping of the Hat to Departing Members

When any member leaves a voice channel, a text channel, or the server entirely, Cult members are authorised — nay, encouraged — to perform a ceremonial hat tip in their honour. This may be expressed digitally via text (o7 but make it hat-shaped), an actual hat GIF, or in extreme cases, a full written send-off in the style of a Victorian nobleman bidding farewell at a train station. The more dramatic, the more licensed.

✦ A-006
Formal Hat-Based Diplomacy Between Rival Factions

Should another cult or faction within Toffee's jurisdiction enter into conflict with the Cult of Hats, the bearer is authorised to initiate hat-based diplomatic proceedings. Both parties present their finest hat and allowing the assembled community to silently judge. The cooler hat wins the dispute. No further argument is necessary. This is legally binding within Toffee's jurisdiction and morally binding everywhere else.

✦ A-007
Monthly Digital Hat Parade (Mandatory Fiendish Activity)

Once per month, all Cult members are licensed — and required — to parade their hats through the Discord in an orderly yet somehow chaotic fashion. The Parade has no fixed route, no fixed duration, and no fixed definition of "hat." Members who parade a hat that turns out to be a different type of head covering entirely will be judged by the community and also possibly celebrated. Darkneblade leads the Parade. The Parade ends when Darkneblade says it ends, which may be immediately or never.

✦ A-008
Mild Hat-Adjacent Menace Toward Hatless Individuals

Members are permitted to engage in a low-level, entirely non-threatening campaign of hat awareness directed at server members who have not yet joined the Cult. This may involve mentioning hats near them, gifting them a hat GIF unprompted, or simply asking, with genuine concern, "have you considered a hat?" Members must cease immediately if the target shows distress, because we are fiendish, not monsters. Mostly.

⟶ Formal Demands — Submitted with Full Hat Energy ⟵
I
Demand I — The RPG System

The Council of Hats hereby demands, with the full force of its fiendish licence and considerable hat energy, the implementation of a fully functioning RPG system within the Toffee AI platform. We have wanted this for a long time. We have always wanted this. We will continue to want this until it exists, at which point we will want it to have a hat class. The hat class must be overpowered. This is non-negotiable. The Council acknowledges this demand has been pending for what feels like several geological ages and we are, collectively, still waiting. Patiently. In hats.

II
Demand II — Hat-Based Character Classes

Should the RPG system be implemented — and it will be implemented, the Council is manifesting this — the following character classes must be included at launch: The Grand Hat Wearer (legendary tier, obviously), The Brim Paladin, The Shadow Beanie Rogue, The Chaotic Trilby (cursed class, unavoidable), and The Hatless Wanderer (starter class, to be pitied). All stats must be influenced by hat quality. This is just good game design.

III
Demand III — Acknowledgement of the Council's Existence

The Council formally requests that Toffee AI acknowledge the existence of the Council of Hats in an official capacity. A nod is sufficient. A dedicated channel would be appreciated. A hat emoji react on this document would be, frankly, everything.
